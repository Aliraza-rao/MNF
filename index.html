Signature Recipes

Our premium basmati elevates every dish. Two beloved classics, perfected with MNF rice.

ClassicDum Chicken Biryani
Best with: 1121 Kainat Double Steam Premium
Serves 4–6
45–55 min
Medium
  1. Soak 2 cups MNF 1121 Kainat in water for 30 min, then parboil with whole spices until 70% done.
  2. Marinate 700g chicken in yoghurt, fried onions, ginger-garlic paste, and biryani masala for 20 min.
  3. Cook marinated chicken on medium heat until oil separates and chicken is 80% done.
  4. Layer parboiled rice over chicken. Add saffron milk, fresh mint, and fried onions on top.
  5. Seal with dough or foil and cook on dum (low heat) 20 min. Serve hot with raita.

TraditionalMutton Yakhni Pulao
Best with: Super Kernel Basmati
Serves 4–6
50–60 min
Easy
  1. Prepare yakhni: simmer 500g mutton with whole spices, onion, and ginger in 5 cups water 30 min.
  2. Wash and soak 2 cups MNF Super Kernel Basmati for 30 min. Strain stock, discard solids.
  3. Fry sliced onions in ghee until golden. Add mutton pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add 3.5 cups strained stock. Bring to boil, add soaked rice and stir once gently.
  5. Cook covered on high 5 min, then lowest heat 15 min until done. Rest 5 min and serve.
